# Support

To report a bug in MK Studio Upkeep, choose **Help → Report a bug…** in the app.
Describe what happened, what you expected and the steps needed to reproduce it.
Review the local preview, then choose **Open GitHub report form** and submit the
report yourself. Diagnostics are optional; nothing is submitted automatically.
A GitHub account is required to submit a report.

Use the [manual](docs/USER_MANUAL.md) for instructions. General questions and
individual setup support are not offered. Please do not contact the maintainer
by email or social media for support. Bug reports and fixes are handled on a
best-effort basis, with no guaranteed response or resolution.

Review every screenshot, log and generated report before sharing. Remove names,
private paths, account information, serial numbers and project content. Never
attach credentials, licences or third-party plugin binaries.

Use the Rights or content concern template for corrections, ownership concerns
or removal requests. Identify the affected material and requested change without
posting confidential evidence. Requests are reviewed; no automatic outcome or
response time is promised.

Security vulnerabilities must not be posted in Issues: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Issues are public. Do not include confidential information in a bug report.

Installation, activation and firmware issues with other software belong with
that software's developer. Product names identify software; they do not imply
affiliation.
